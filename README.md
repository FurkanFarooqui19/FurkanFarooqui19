I'm an engineer in Mumbai building at the intersection of **information retrieval**, **applied AI**, and **modern web systems**. Currently pursuing B.E. Computer Engineering at Mumbai University (2023–2027) and seeking full-time **SDE & Applied AI roles**.

My work spans from a **3.3 ms p50 BM25 search engine** evaluated on Cranfield IR benchmarks, to **multimodal Gemini Vision** OCR pipelines for clinical data extraction, to high-throughput **FastAPI microservices** and **React 19 + Next.js 14** frontends — all engineered from first principles and deployed to production.
